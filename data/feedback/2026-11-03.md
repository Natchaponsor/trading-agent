## What worked

- Both picks reached their buy zones (2 of 2 entered). No sign that buy zones are set too low.
- No stop was hit early. NVDA held 11 trading days before its stop (entry 2026-10-08, exit 2026-10-23). VRTX never hit its stop.
- The one pick that met every rule (VRTX, R/R 2.09) hit its target. The buy-zone alert fired on 2026-10-15 at 509.03.
- The picker held to the rules in week 2026-11-02. It refused the technical agent's hand-made "adjusted" targets because they were not script output.

## What did not

- **No sell alerts were sent for either exit.** data/alert_log.csv has only two rows, both buy_zone alerts. NVDA hit its stop on 2026-10-23 and VRTX hit its target on 2026-10-30, but neither appears in the log. The cause is in scripts/price_alert.py: a pick is only watched while `valid_until` has not passed (`picks.get("valid_until", "9999") >= today`). NVDA stopped being watched after 2026-10-09 and VRTX after 2026-10-16. Both exits came later. Also, picks.json only holds the current week, so older picks drop out when a new week is written. If Top bought either stock, no alert told him to sell. This is the biggest process problem found so far. It is a code fix, not a strategy.json setting (see below).
- **Few rule-based picks.** Over the 3 weeks that ran, there were 6 sector slots. They produced 1 rule-based pick (VRTX) and 1 approved exception (NVDA). In week 2026-11-02, no stock in either sector reached R/R 2.0 on scan levels. The best was VRTX at 1.78. This is the pattern the 2026-10-11 review said to watch for.
- The only pick below the 2.0 floor (NVDA at 1.58) lost. One trade is not evidence either way, but it does not support lowering the floor.

## Proposed changes

No changes to config/strategy.json this week.

Reasons:
- enough_data is false (2 closed picks). Weights and score settings cannot be judged.
- The process checks that strategy.json controls look fine. Buy zones were reached (2 of 2) and stops were not hit early (0 of 2 within 2 days).
- The low pick rate is real, but the only fix inside strategy.json would be lowering `technical.min_reward_risk` (now 2.0). The one trade below 2.0 lost, and the one at 2.0 won. That gives no evidence for lowering it. The technical agent's idea of using 1.272 extension targets would change how scripts set levels. That needs a change in scripts/indicators.py with a test, plus Top's approval. It is not a settings change.

Outside strategy.json (for Top to decide, not a settings change):
- Fix scripts/price_alert.py so a pick that was entered keeps being watched for its target and stop until it exits, or until `performance.max_holding_trading_days` (20) runs out. That matches how performance.py scores picks. One option is to read open picks from data/picks_history.jsonl and data/alert_state.json, not only from data/picks.json. Add a test in tests/.

Watch next weeks:

- Whether picks enter late in their window, and whether any never enter.

## Data quality warnings

1. **Missed sell alerts (see above).** The alert log can't be used to check exits, because sell events were never checked after valid_until.
2. **Two weeks were not run.** There is no data/weekly/2026-10-19 or 2026-10-26 folder and no picks for those weeks. That means fewer chances to collect results.
3. **Week 2026-11-02 used old prices.** Its scan ran 2026-10-27 during market hours, and picks.json was created 2026-10-27T10:22. That is 6 days before the week began, during a busy earnings and Fed week. It had no picks, so no score was affected. Future weeks should scan close to the Sunday run.
4. **An empty folder data/weekly/2026-11-09 exists.** That week has not started. It is harmless, but it should be removed or filled by the real run.
5. **Returns use the middle of the buy zone, not the alert price.** performance.py treats entry as (entry_low + entry_high) / 2. NVDA's alert price was 230.57, and VRTX's was 509.03. Real results from those prices would be different. Logging real fills in my_trades.csv fixes this.
6. **Entry day can differ from the alert day.** performance.py marks VRTX as entered 2026-10-12 because the daily low touched the zone. The first alert came on 2026-10-15, because alerts only check twice a day.
7. **Exception picks are still mixed into the totals.** NVDA was an approved exception (R/R 1.58). Without it, the rule-based record is 1 closed pick: 1 win, +10.52%. This was flagged on 2026-10-11 and is still not handled in performance.py.
8. **Mixed strategy versions.** NVDA is version 1 and VRTX is version 2. The only v1 to v2 change was the alert windows, so scoring is not affected.
9. **my_trades.csv is empty.** There is no record of whether Top bought NVDA or VRTX, so real results can't be compared with the picks. Please log any trades, including the smaller NVDA position if one was taken.
10. **Uncommitted alert code.** scripts/price_alert.py (watchlist support) and tests/test_core.py have local changes that are not committed. GitHub Actions runs the committed version, so watchlist alerts are not live until these changes are committed.
