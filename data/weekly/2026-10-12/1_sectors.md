## Choices

**1. Technology (XLK).** It is the only eligible sector with a positive score (2.66). The ETF is ahead of QQQ on all three timeframes and is above its 50-day average. There is real news this week: ASML and TSMC report on Oct 14 (Saxo), and Saxo cites TSMC's September revenue up 54.6% year over year. Those results will test the AI chip story directly. Caution: the median Nasdaq-100 tech member trails QQQ over 3 months (-0.67), so a few large names are still doing most of the work. Also, the score fell from 4.46 last week to 2.66, so the lead is getting smaller. Saxo also flags doubts about AI demand after reports that OpenAI's revenue missed expectations.

**2. Health Care (XLV).** This is the #2 score (-4.02). It is still behind QQQ overall, but it improved a lot from -10.25 last week. The ETF is now above its 50-day average and slightly ahead of QQQ over 3 months (0.56). Johnson & Johnson and UnitedHealth report this week (Kitco, Saxo). Health Care is also more defensive if CPI comes in hot and rate-hike bets come back. The weak spot is the members' 6-month median (-23.25). The Nasdaq-100 health care names have lagged badly over the longer run.

**Swap decision:** No swap. Consumer Staples (#3, -9.94) is below its 50-day average and trails QQQ on every timeframe. I found no news this week that clearly favors it over Health Care.

## Risks to watch

- **CPI on Wednesday, Oct 14, then PPI on Thursday.** Kitco expects September CPI at 3.6% a year, with core at about 2.5%. A hot number could bring back bets on a rate hike at the Oct 27-28 Fed meeting, and that would hurt high-valuation tech the most.
- **Yields.** ^TNX is at 5.24 and still rising. Kitco reports the 10-year recently hit 24-year highs. One analyst quoted there: "At some point, major indices are going to cry uncle on higher rates."
- **Chip earnings risk.** ASML and TSMC report Oct 14. A miss or weak guidance would hit the Technology pick directly. Saxo flags concern about AI demand.
- **Narrow market.** Small caps (IWM -2.79% in 1 month) and markets outside the US (EFA -2.14%) are lagging. Gains that rest on a few names can reverse quickly.
- **Holiday.** Oct 12 is Columbus Day. Stocks trade, but the cash Treasury market is closed (CMC Markets week-ahead, via search summary).
- **Fed speaker.** Fed Chair Warsh speaks at the IMF/World Bank meetings on Fri, Oct 16 (Saxo).
