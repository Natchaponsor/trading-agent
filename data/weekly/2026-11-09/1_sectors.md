## Big picture

The US market is in a pullback, led by tech. The Nasdaq-100 (QQQ) is down 9.41% over 1 month and 4.4% over 3 months, though it is still above its 200-day average. Reuters says it closed Monday more than 10% below its record high, which counts as a correction. The S&P 500 held up better (down 2.75% in a month), and small caps (IWM) are down 1.17% in a month. Global markets are mixed: developed markets (EFA) are about flat for the month (down 0.39%), but emerging markets (EEM) fell 10.85% and are below their 200-day average. Bond yields eased this month but are still high: the 10-year yield (^TNX) is 4.94, down 7.04% over 1 month but up 6.93% over 3 months, and long bonds (TLT) rose 5.17% in a month. Fear is now high: VIX is 20.87, just above 20, and up 34.47% in a month.

## Risks to watch

- **Tech rebound.** Both picks win mainly because tech is falling. Morgan Stanley (via Reuters) said the midterm elections should remove a major overhang and that the correction is in its "final innings." If AI stocks bounce hard, defensive sectors will likely trail QQQ, even if they stay positive.
- **Midterm elections, Tue Nov 3.** Reuters says polls favor Democrats to win the House, with the Senate uncertain. Reuters also says health care could be sensitive to the results, since drug pricing and insurance policy can change with Congress.
- **AMD earnings, Tue Nov 3 after the close.** A strong report could lift chip stocks and cut the lead of defensives.
- **Jobs report, Fri Nov 6, and CPI inflation, Tue Nov 10.** A hot reading would raise December hike odds. That would hurt rate-sensitive groups like Staples and Utilities, but it would hurt high-valuation tech too.
- **Oil and the Middle East.** Reuters reports oil rose again after a US strike on Iran's Qeshm Island, and an Iranian official said a deal to end the war is far off. Higher costs can squeeze staples company profits.
- **Weak world markets.** Emerging markets (EEM) fell 10.85% in a month and are below their 200-day average. Fear (VIX 20.87) is above 20.
