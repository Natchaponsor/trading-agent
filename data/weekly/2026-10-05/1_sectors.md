## Risks to watch

- **Yields keep rising.** ^TNX is at 5.28 and rising. Higher discount rates hurt high-valuation tech the most, so this is the main threat to the Technology pick.
- **Fed.** The next meeting is Oct 28, with more than 60% odds of a hike (Kitco, Sept 25). Any inflation data that firms up those odds could hit tech.
- **Narrow market.** The S&P 500 equal-weight index fell about 4% in September, and 9 of 11 sectors were negative (Kitco). Gains that depend on a few names can reverse quickly.
- **Health Care momentum is weak.** Its members' median trails QQQ by -14.68 over 1 month. If tech keeps rallying, this pick will likely keep lagging.
- **Upcoming calendar.** September CPI is reportedly due Oct 14, and Q3 earnings season reportedly starts around Oct 13 with JPMorgan. Both fall just after this week. I did not confirm any major scheduled US data for the week of Oct 5-9.
