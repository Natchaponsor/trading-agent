Fear is low but rising: VIX is 17.75, below 20, but up 10.45% in a month.

## Choices

**1. Health Care (XLV).** It has the top score (2.71), up from -4.02 in the Oct 12 report. The ETF beat QQQ by 6.66 points over 1 month and 15.62 points over 6 months, and the typical Nasdaq-100 health care stock beat QQQ by 7.91 points over 1 month. It also held up during the October chip selloff, which makes it a steadier choice if AI stocks stay shaky. Eli Lilly reports soon (Reuters/Kitco lists it for next week; one calendar says Oct 29). Lilly is not a Nasdaq-100 stock, but it moves the whole health care group. Weak spot: over 3 months the sector still trails QQQ (ETF -0.95, members -2.37).

**2. Technology (XLK).** It is the only other eligible sector with a positive score (0.82). The ETF is ahead of QQQ over 3 months (4.83) and 6 months (10.46) and is above its 50-day average. Chip stocks are bouncing: Reuters reports the chip index up 1.8% today, its third straight daily gain. The coming two weeks will decide this pick. Alphabet and Microsoft report Wednesday (Oct 28), Amazon on Thursday, and AMD on Nov 3 after the close (AMD press release). Caution: the score keeps falling (4.46, then 2.66 in the Oct 12 report, now 0.82). The typical tech member trails QQQ over 1 month (-1.44) and 3 months (-2.37), so a few large names are carrying the sector. Meta and Lam Research already disappointed investors this season, and Reuters says the market is "just not giving the benefit of the doubt to companies that are spending a ton of money" on AI.

**Swap decision:** No swap. Consumer Staples (#3, score -3.35) has a strong last month (8.02 for the ETF), but the typical member trails QQQ by 13.87 points over 3 months. I found no news this week that clearly favors Staples over Technology. If tech earnings disappoint this week, Staples is the first sector to reconsider next week.

## Risks to watch

- **Fed decision, Wed Oct 28.** Reuters says futures priced about a 90% chance of a hold at the current 3.75%-4.00% range, but markets still see about an 80% chance of a hike in December. A hawkish tone from Chair Warsh would hurt high-valuation tech the most.
- **Megacap earnings this week.** Alphabet and Microsoft (Wed), Amazon (Thu). Investors are judging AI spending (hyperscaler capex near $800 billion this year, per Reuters) and whether it pays off. Weak guidance would hit the Technology pick directly.
- **PCE inflation, Thu Oct 29.** Economists expect it to rise to 3.6% from 3.4% (Reuters). A hot number would revive hike bets.
- **Midterm elections, Tue Nov 3**, the same day AMD reports. Reuters reports investors see risk to the AI trade from a data-center backlash.
- **Oil and the Middle East.** Brent is near its highest level since late April (Reuters). Rising fuel costs are feeding inflation.
- **Narrow market.** Small caps (IWM -2.77% over 3 months) and emerging markets (EEM -6.89% in 1 month, below the 200-day) are weak.
